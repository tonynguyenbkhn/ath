WHO WE ARE ?

ATH is a performing arts hub based in Hanoi. We use performing arts as a tool for human development, creativity, and connection.

ATH brings together artistic creation, education, and social engagement in one place, working with individuals, schools, and organizations.

“Art is not a reflection of life, but a way of transforming it.” – Konstantin Stanislavski

WHAT WE DO?

  • Artistic programs and live performances
  • Educational and training programs
  • Theatre-based projects for communities and organisations
