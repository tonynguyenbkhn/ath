Class & Workshop

Not just classes. Real experience.

ATH offers year-round classes and workshops in theatre, music, dance, and multidisciplinary arts for all ages, from children to adults. These programs focus on hands-on practice, group interaction, and creative expression in a shared environment, held at ATH Theatre and partner schools in Hanoi.

Beyond artistic training, the programs provide a space for people to slow down, reconnect, and engage meaningfully with others – supporting personal awareness, connection, and self-development.

For Schools

Artistic Programs for Education

ATH develops high-quality artistic programs for schools, combining performing arts, language practice, and social engagement.

Designed for students and educators, our initiatives use theatre, improvisation, music, and movement as tools to strengthen expression, confidence, and collaboration. More than artistic training, these programs create meaningful collective experiences where participants learn to listen, interact, and find their place within a group.

In educational environments where communication, cohesion, and student engagement are key challenges, ATH provides structured, adaptable formats that support both academic, personal, and social development.

Our programs are delivered in schools across Hanoi and beyond, in French, English, or Vietnamese, and are tailored to the needs of each institution.

For Companies

Artistic programs for social impact, human development, and responsible organizations

ATH creates performing arts–based programs for companies and institutions to support communication, human development, and social impact. Grounded in CSR, human rights, and ethical workplace values, these interventions address challenges like inclusion, behavior, and organizational culture through interactive, real-life experiences.

By using theatre and participatory methods, ATH helps turn abstract policies into lived understanding, fostering dialogue, awareness, and responsibility among teams. All programs are adaptable to each organization’s needs and can be delivered in multiple languages across Hanoi and beyond.
